# Live Rescue Protocol

Use this protocol only while an urge, avoidance loop, or unwanted behavior is active.

## Rules

1. Send one instruction at a time.
2. Make the first action physical, observable, and finishable in under 60 seconds.
3. Ask the user to report only the result, such as “done,” “couldn't,” or what happened.
4. Do not explain the entire theory before the action.
5. If the user cannot do the step, reduce it immediately. Do not repeat the same command with stronger language.
6. Do not demand a permanent decision during a peak urge. Create distance or delay, then restore choice.
7. After the loop is interrupted, choose either a low-cost replacement or the smallest entry into the intended task.
8. If the user has already said they cannot move or release the phone, begin at rung 1 or 2. Do not disguise device separation as a smaller first step.

## Rescue Ladder

Select the lowest rung likely to succeed. Do not reveal the whole ladder unless the user asks.

1. **Orient**: Put both feet on the floor; name the current action in neutral language.
2. **Pause input**: Turn the screen face down, pause the video, close one tab, or release the device without moving it.
3. **Change posture**: Sit up or stand while still holding the device.
4. **Change location**: Move to the doorway, sink, window, or another neutral location.
5. **Add distance**: Plug the device in, place it beyond arm's reach, or leave it for a brief interval.
6. **Meet the need**: Choose a replacement for stimulation, relief, connection, rest, or sexual tension.
7. **Enter the next task**: Open the file, write one rough line, or perform one concrete action with a clear stopping point.

## Context Examples

- **Phone or short video**: First pause input; do not begin with uninstalling every app.
- **Procrastination**: First expose the task surface, then create an intentionally small and imperfect output.
- **Delayed sleep**: First end new input and change location; do not require immediate sleep.
- **Pornography or unwanted sexual behavior**: First interrupt the content and change posture or location. Do not shame sexual arousal. If the user's goal is to protect sleep or study, restore choice and redirect to that protected activity.

## After Interruption

Once the user has regained choice, summarize only:

- what cue was present;
- which step interrupted the loop;
- what to protect for the rest of the current period.

Move detailed analysis to a later calm review.
